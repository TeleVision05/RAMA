# Asset credits

Most of RAMA is generated by this project. Eight files are not: the humanoid base
meshes and their animation clips come from two free asset packs released under
**CC0 1.0** (public-domain dedication). CC0 allows copying, modifying and redistributing
them, commercially or not, with no attribution required. They are credited here anyway.

## How the files were matched

The files in this repo are conversions, not byte copies, so hashes alone do not match.
What was compared:

- **Character meshes.** The glTF `asset.generator` of all four is `FBX2glTF v0.9.7`,
  and their mesh names (`King_Body`, `Farmer_Pants`, `Medieval_Head`, ...), material
  names and `CharacterArmature` bone names (`Hips`, `Abdomen`, `Torso`, `Index4.L`, ...)
  are those of the Quaternius FBX files. Every bind-pose vertex of each model was
  compared with the vertices of the matching pack FBX after normalising scale and axis:
  100% of points coincide within 0.2% of the model's height in both directions, for all
  four models (King and Farmer also match the pack's glTF and the Poly Pizza `.glb`
  exactly).
- **Animation clips.** Each file has the same 23 `Rig_Medium` bone names and the same
  clip list (11, 15, 22 and 20 clips) as the KayKit 1.1 file of the same category. The
  repo copies were re-exported with Blender 4.3 without the pack's mannequin mesh, which
  shifts every key by one frame (1/30 s) and rotates the `root` bone by 90° (axis
  change). Allowing for that, 65 of the 68 clips match the pack within 1.1° on every
  bone at every sampled time; `Spawn_Air`, `Spawn_Ground` and `Running_B` differ more,
  consistent with revisions between pack versions. The game uses none of those three.
